# Logistics Memo - Yantian

Logistics Memo ID: LOG-2026-0026
Port: Yantian
Country: China
Region: APAC
Congestion Level: high

The logistics control tower reports high congestion at Yantian. Shipments from this port
should be reviewed for components used in tier_1 products and for suppliers with high capacity
utilization. Delays can cascade into production risk when there is single-source component coverage.

## Control-Tower Notes

The port risk is most important for shipments with near-term production demand, premium freight exposure, or components used by multiple finished products. The control tower recommends comparing all delayed shipments from Yantian against BOM rows where single_source_flag is yes.

## Response Guidance

- Review supplier scorecards for origin-country suppliers.
- Check shipments with risk_flag yes and delay_days greater than seven.
- Ask planning to identify products with less than two weeks of component coverage.
- Use alternate carriers only for tier_1 products or customer-critical orders.
