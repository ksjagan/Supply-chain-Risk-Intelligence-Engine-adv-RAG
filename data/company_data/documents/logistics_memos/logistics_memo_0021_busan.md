# Logistics Memo - Busan

Logistics Memo ID: LOG-2026-0021
Port: Busan
Country: South Korea
Region: APAC
Congestion Level: moderate

The logistics control tower reports moderate congestion at Busan. Shipments from this port
should be reviewed for components used in tier_1 products and for suppliers with high capacity
utilization. Delays can cascade into production risk when there is single-source component coverage.

## Control-Tower Notes

The port risk is most important for shipments with near-term production demand, premium freight exposure, or components used by multiple finished products. The control tower recommends comparing all delayed shipments from Busan against BOM rows where single_source_flag is yes.

## Response Guidance

- Review supplier scorecards for origin-country suppliers.
- Check shipments with risk_flag yes and delay_days greater than seven.
- Ask planning to identify products with less than two weeks of component coverage.
- Use alternate carriers only for tier_1 products or customer-critical orders.
