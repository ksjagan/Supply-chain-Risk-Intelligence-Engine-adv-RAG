# Market Report - power electronics

Market Report ID: MKT-2026-0015
Category: power electronics
Market Signal: allocation pressure

Market intelligence indicates allocation pressure for power electronics. Procurement should evaluate contract coverage,
supplier concentration, and exposure to high-margin products. The risk is highest where critical components
have no approved alternate supplier and shipment lead times are already increasing.

## Procurement Implications

The category team should review supplier concentration, open purchase orders, and BOM criticality before negotiating the next allocation cycle. power electronics risk can become product revenue risk when demand is firm but supply recovery requires qualification lead time.

## Recommended Actions

- Identify critical and high components in this category.
- Review products with annual revenue above 250M USD that depend on those components.
- Check whether alternate suppliers are approved and production-ready.
- Add contract clauses for expedite support, allocation transparency, and quality containment.
