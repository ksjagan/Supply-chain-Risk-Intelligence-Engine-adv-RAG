# Risk Report - Mexico connectivity module

Report ID: RISK-2026-0103
Region: North America
Country: Mexico
Port: Manzanillo
Category: connectivity module
Risk Theme: capacity shortage
Severity: elevated

The current risk outlook for connectivity module supply from Mexico is elevated. Analysts cite
capacity shortage as the primary driver. Suppliers shipping through Manzanillo may experience variability in
transit time, customs release, and production scheduling.

## Analyst Assessment

Risk is most material for suppliers with high capacity utilization, low on-time delivery, or components that map to single-source BOM rows. The practical signal to monitor is whether shipment delays, supplier scorecard deterioration, and compliance exceptions appear together for the same supplier or category.

## Exposure Pattern

- Region affected: North America
- Country affected: Mexico
- Port dependency: Manzanillo
- Component category: connectivity module
- Primary risk driver: capacity shortage
- Severity call: elevated

## Recommended Actions

- Increase safety stock for critical connectivity module components.
- Validate alternate supplier readiness for products with high revenue contribution.
- Review shipments from Manzanillo with delay risk flags.
- Prioritize high-revenue products with single-source exposure.
- Recheck procurement contracts for premium freight and recovery clauses.
