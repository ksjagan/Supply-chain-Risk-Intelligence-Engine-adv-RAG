# Risk Report - China sensor

Report ID: RISK-2026-0025
Region: APAC
Country: China
Port: Yantian
Category: sensor
Risk Theme: port congestion
Severity: moderate

The current risk outlook for sensor supply from China is moderate. Analysts cite
port congestion as the primary driver. Suppliers shipping through Yantian may experience variability in
transit time, customs release, and production scheduling.

## Analyst Assessment

Risk is most material for suppliers with high capacity utilization, low on-time delivery, or components that map to single-source BOM rows. The practical signal to monitor is whether shipment delays, supplier scorecard deterioration, and compliance exceptions appear together for the same supplier or category.

## Exposure Pattern

- Region affected: APAC
- Country affected: China
- Port dependency: Yantian
- Component category: sensor
- Primary risk driver: port congestion
- Severity call: moderate

## Recommended Actions

- Increase safety stock for critical sensor components.
- Validate alternate supplier readiness for products with high revenue contribution.
- Review shipments from Yantian with delay risk flags.
- Prioritize high-revenue products with single-source exposure.
- Recheck procurement contracts for premium freight and recovery clauses.
